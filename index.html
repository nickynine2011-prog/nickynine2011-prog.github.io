About

I'm a medical student at the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland, Medical University of Bahrain. Before medicine I studied health sciences at Brock University in Canada, where I took coursework in epidemiology, research methods, chemistry and physiology.

Those years taught me to read research critically and to treat every clinical question as something to look up, test and check. I bring that habit into medicine, alongside a growing interest in procedural skills and surgery.
