May 2023

May 2023 · Poster

Supporting the Evaluation of Vision Screening Clinics in Niagara

A student quality improvement project with Niagara Region Public Health on its school vision screening clinics. The team revised the information sent to parents and observed the clinics with a structured flow tool: younger children needed more guidance, and queues ran long. I drafted the context section of the final report.

Mrezar J, Di Salvo K, Lucisano C, Moosapeta N, Law M, Lowes C, Butchey N, Kelm K. Poster presented at Niagara Health Research Day, Brock University, St. Catharines, 4 May 2023.

Brock University’s Interprofessional Education for Quality Improvement Program (I-EQUIP), with Niagara Region Public Health and the Essilor Vision Foundation. Supervisor: Dr. Madelyn Law.
