I’m a second-year medical student at RCSI Bahrain, originally from Hamilton, Ontario. I completed an Honours BSc in Medical Sciences at Brock University in 2023 with first-class standing.

Before starting medicine I worked for eleven months as a medical secretary at Stonechurch Family Health Centre, a teaching practice in the McMaster Family Health Team. Earlier, I volunteered in emergency, palliative care, dialysis and rehabilitation settings, shadowed family and emergency physicians, and co-authored a quality improvement poster.

I’m interested in internal medicine, primary care and patient safety, and I’m glad to hear about elective and research opportunities in Canada and the United States.
